# Atomic capability ledger

Every distinct capability extracted from the nine sources, with stable ID, provenance, disposition, destination, and test coverage.

**Conventions.**
- Each row's *Requirement* is the behavioral statement; it includes its trigger condition ("when auditing…", "before closing…"). Inputs/outputs are those of the destination workflow/template unless stated in the row.
- *Sources* cite exact files. Abbreviations — **S1** Lespinasse article · **S2** rlespinasse/agent-skills (`sk/` = `skills/diataxis/`) · **S3** keithpatton/diataxis-agent-skill (`dx/` = `diataxis/`) · **S4** peterknego/diataxis-docs-skill (`sk/` = `skills/diataxis-docs/`) · **S5** marketplace listing · **S6** sammcj/agentic-coding (`dd/` = `Skills_disabled/diataxis-documentation/`) · **S7** anivar/developer-docs-framework · **S8** diataxis.fr (`source/*.rst`) · **S9** docs-and-release-rig (files by number, e.g. `09` = `3-security-methods/09-…`).
- *O* — overlap class: **U** unique · **O** overlapping (canonical + contributors merged) · **C** complementary · **X** was conflicting (resolution in research-report §5).
- *Disp* — disposition: **R** retained · **M** merged · **A** adapted · **Op** optionalized. (No exclusions; see foot of file.)
- *Destination* paths are relative to the skill root. *Test* IDs refer to `tests/evals.json`.
